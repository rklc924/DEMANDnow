The **Calendar** page collects every date across your matters: payment deadlines, next steps, limitation dates, builders lien deadlines, rent arrears dates from the [Landlord section](/help/landlord) and collected debts.

# Using the calendar

- The three tiles at the top show **overdue items**, items **due in the next 30 days**, and what you've **collected** so far. Click a tile to filter the list below.
- The month grid shows each day's items. Use the arrows to move between months.
- The list under the grid can be filtered to **All**, **Overdue**, **Upcoming** or **Collected**.
- **Add all to my calendar (.ics)** downloads every date as one file you can import into Outlook, Google Calendar or Apple Calendar.

# Reminders in your own calendar

When you connect Outlook / Microsoft 365 or Gmail / Google in [Settings](/help/settings), every demand letter you send adds follow-up reminders to that calendar:

1. **Payment deadline:** the day the letter's deadline to pay falls.
2. **Next step:** the day after, a reminder to start the claim, or to send the file to a lawyer for larger debts.
3. **Filing check** (Small Claims only): 14 days after the deadline, a check that the Notice of Claim is filed and served.
4. **Limitation warning:** 60 days before the limitation date.

Each reminder links back to the matter. You can turn reminders off in Settings. If you use a different calendar app, use **Download .ics** on the matter page.
