**Settings** controls how your letters look, who they come from and when they go out. Only admins can open it.

# Letterhead

Upload your letterhead as a one-page PDF (best) or a full-page PNG or JPG. The letter text is placed below the top 2 inches, so keep your header in that band. A green **Letterhead on file** tag confirms it's saved. Until you upload one, letters use a plain page.

# Sending rules

- **Auto-send:** when ticked, demand letters go out automatically to **business** customers once an invoice passes the threshold, during the nightly sync. Consumer customers always wait for your review.
- **Threshold (days past due):** when an invoice joins the demand queue. The default is 90 days, and the minimum is 30.
- **Days to pay after the letter:** the deadline the letter gives. The default is 14 days, and the minimum is 7.

Click **Save rules**.

# Company details on the letter

Enter your **legal business name**, **phone**, the person the letter is **signed by** and their **title**, a **reply email**, and **payment instructions** (for example, e-transfer to your accounts email or a cheque payable to your company). These appear on every letter and on court forms. The jurisdiction is British Columbia.

# Email and calendar

Connect **Outlook / Microsoft 365** or **Gmail / Google** once, and:

- demand letters are emailed from your own address with the PDF attached the moment they're sent, and
- follow-up reminders are added to your calendar. See [Calendar and reminders](/help/calendar).

You can turn either option off and click **Save**, or **Disconnect** the mailbox at any time. Using another email app? Letters still go out from Demand Now, and each matter has an .ics file you can add to any calendar.

# AI assistants

Connect Claude or ChatGPT to start demands from a chat. See [Claude and ChatGPT](/help/ai-assistants).
