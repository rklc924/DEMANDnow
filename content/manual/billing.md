# How the letter limit works

Each demand letter you send counts as one letter in the calendar month it's sent. The count resets on the first of the month. The header shows how many you've used, and Billing shows a progress bar.

On Standard, once you've used all {{STANDARD_LIMIT}} letters, sending is paused until next month, and auto-send holds back any further letters. Upgrade to Unlimited to keep sending.

# Change or cancel your plan

- **Manage billing & invoices** opens Stripe's secure portal, where you can switch plans, update your card, download invoices and cancel.
- **Cancel plan** cancels from inside Demand Now. Your existing matters stay available, but sending is paused until you choose a plan again.

# Card on file and one-time charges

Your card is used for your plan and anything you book: process servers, company searches, lien filings and title searches. You're never charged without choosing something first. **Update card** replaces it.

One-time charges are listed under **One-time charges** with the date, what it was for and the amount.
