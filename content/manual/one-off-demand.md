Only have one unpaid invoice? You can send a single demand letter for **{{ONE_OFF_PRICE}}** without subscribing. It works for British Columbia debts only.

# How it works

1. Go to [One-off demand](/start). You don't need an account to start.
2. Fill in **your business** (legal name, who signs, phone, email, how to pay you, and optionally your letterhead), **who owes you** (name, business or individual, mailing address, email) and **the debt** (invoice number, amount, due date, invoice date, interest rate in your terms, and what it was for).
3. Click **Continue**. Your details are saved and you see where the claim would go if it stays unpaid.
4. Choose how to continue:
   - **Pay {{ONE_OFF_PRICE}}** for this one demand. You get the letter on your letterhead and the full step-by-step guide for the claim, including the Notice of Claim details for Small Claims debts.
   - **Subscribe** if you have more overdue invoices. This demand carries over to your new account.
5. After payment, download the letter. Send it by email and registered mail and keep proof of delivery. If you're not paid by the deadline, follow the guide.

> **Tip:** Keep the link to your one-off demand page. It's how you get back to your letter and guide.

If the debt looks older than the {{LIMITATION_YEARS}}-year limitation period, the page warns you to speak to a lawyer before sending a demand.
