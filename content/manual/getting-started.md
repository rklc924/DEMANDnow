Demand Now helps BC businesses collect overdue invoices. The software finds invoices that are more than 90 days past due, generates a demand letter on your letterhead, and walks you through each next step if the debt stays unpaid. This chapter gets your account ready in about ten minutes.

> **Tip:** Demand Now is software, not a law firm. It generates letters and fills in court forms from the details you give it, and gives you legal information about BC procedure. If you want advice on a particular dispute, use the **Request lawyer review** option on any matter.

# 1. Create your account

1. Go to [Sign up](/signup) and enter your name, your business name (as it should appear on your letters), your work email and a password of at least 10 characters.
2. Check your inbox for a confirmation email and click the link. You can look around before you confirm, but you can't send demand letters until your email is confirmed. If the email doesn't arrive, use **Resend the link** in the yellow banner on the Receivables page.

# 2. Add a card on file

Every Demand Now account keeps a card on file. It pays for your plan and for anything you book, such as a process server, a company search or a lien filing. You're never charged without choosing something first.

1. After you sign up you're taken to **Add a card to your account**.
2. Click **Add card securely**. You enter the card on Stripe's secure page, and the card details never touch Demand Now's servers.
3. You can replace the card any time from **Billing > Update card**.

# 4. Set up your company

Open **Settings** and do three things before you send your first letter:

1. **Upload your letterhead.** A one-page PDF works best. The letter text starts 2 inches from the top of the page, so keep your logo and header in that band.
2. **Fill in the company details on the letter:** your legal business name, the person who signs, their title, a phone number, a reply email and how customers should pay you.
3. **Connect your email** (Outlook / Microsoft 365 or Gmail / Google) so letters go out from your own mailbox and follow-up dates land in your calendar.

[Company settings](/help/settings) explains every option.

# 5. Get your invoices in

Your overdue invoices appear on the [Receivables dashboard](/help/receivables). You can also add invoices by hand with **+ Add invoice**. Demand Now tracks them and starts the demand workflow once they pass your threshold.

# Signing in and passwords

- Sign in at [Log in](/login) with your email and password.
- Forgot your password? Choose **Forgot your password?** on the login page and we'll email you a reset link. Changing your password signs you out on every other device.
