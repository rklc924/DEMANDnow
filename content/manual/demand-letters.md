Each overdue invoice opens as a **matter**. The matter page has everything for that debt: who owes you, the demand letter, the next steps and the deadlines. Open one by clicking an invoice on the Receivables page.

# Step 1: Confirm who you're pursuing

At the top of every matter is **Who you're pursuing: legal name and address**. Confirm it before you send the letter.

Your accounting system might call a customer "Harbourview" when their legal name is "Harbourview Renovations Ltd.". A judgment is only as good as the name on it. If the name is wrong you might win but be unable to garnish wages, seize a bank account or register against property. Service at the wrong address may not count either, and the limitation clock keeps running while you fix it.

**If the debtor is a company:**

1. Look for a name ending in Ltd., Inc., Corp. or Limited, or a number company like "1234567 B.C. Ltd.", on the contract, credit application or their invoices.
2. Get a BC Registry company search. It shows the exact legal name, the incorporation number, whether the company is active, the registered and records office addresses and the directors.
3. You can **order a search** from the matter page for {{COMPANY_SEARCH_FEE}} plus BC Registries fees, charged to your card, usually within one business day. Or **upload a search** you already have, and Demand Now reads it and fills in the details for you to check.
4. If the company is dissolved, in liquidation or in receivership, stop and request lawyer review.

**If the debtor is a person or sole proprietor:**

1. Use their full legal name, not a nickname or initials.
2. If they trade under an unincorporated business name, name the person, for example "Jane Smith, doing business as Smith Renovations".
3. Check [OrgBook BC](https://www.orgbook.gov.bc.ca) to make sure the name isn't actually a company.
4. Confirm where they live now.

Then open **Enter and confirm the details**, fill in the form, note how you confirmed it, and click **Confirm these details**. Your letters, court forms and process server booking all use this name and address. If something looks wrong, a red box lists what to check.

# Step 2: Review the letter

Click **Open PDF** to see the letter exactly as it will go out, on your letterhead and signed by the person in your company details. The software generates the letter from the invoice and your settings. It states the amount owing, any contractual interest, how to pay, and the pay-by deadline (14 days unless you change it in Settings).

If anything is wrong, fix it at the source: the customer or invoice details, or your company details in [Settings](/help/settings). Then open the PDF again.

# Step 3: Send it

Click **Send demand letter**. If your mailbox is connected, the letter is emailed from your own address with the PDF attached. The matter page then shows when it was sent, how, and the pay-by deadline.

When a letter is sent, Demand Now also:

- counts it as one letter against your plan for the month,
- adds follow-up reminders: the payment deadline, the next step the day after, a filing check for Small Claims matters, and a warning 60 days before the limitation date. They go into your connected calendar, and you can always **Download .ics** for any other calendar app.

> **Tip:** Keep proof the letter was delivered, such as a delivery receipt or a registered mail tracking print-out. Courts and the CRT ask what you did to resolve the debt, and the checklist asks you to upload it.

You can't send if your email isn't confirmed, you have no active plan, or you've used your monthly letters on Standard. The message on the page tells you which.

# Who the matter is assigned to

The **Assigned to** box at the top of the matter shows who is working on it. That person gets an email when someone else moves the matter forward. Admins, and regular users their admin allows, can pick someone else and click **Assign**. See [Your team and user roles](/help/team).

On a team account, some buttons on this page only appear if your admin allows them: **Send demand letter**, **Mark paid**, **Delete invoice** and **Request lawyer review**. If **Request lawyer review** isn't available to you, ask your admin.

# When they pay

If they paid the whole balance, click **Mark paid**. The matter closes, the workflow stops, upcoming reminders are removed, and the matter shows as collected on your [Calendar](/help/calendar).

If they paid only part, or paid on a different day, use **Funds collected** at the bottom of the checklist instead. See [Funds collected](/help/enforcement#funds-collected).

# Banners you may see

- **Limitation date is close:** fewer than 120 days remain to start a claim.
- **Added from Claude / ChatGPT:** an AI assistant created this matter. Check the legal name, address and amount before you send.
- **Delivery problem:** the email couldn't be delivered. Check the customer's email address and send another way.
