You can connect Claude or ChatGPT to your Demand Now account. Then you can ask the assistant about an unpaid invoice, and it can tell you where the claim would go and add the matter to Demand Now for you to review and send.

> **Important:** An assistant can't send letters, file anything, charge your card or see your password. You review and send every letter in Demand Now.

# Connect Claude

1. In Demand Now, open **Settings** and copy the **Connector URL** under **AI assistants**.
2. In Claude, open **Settings**, then **Connectors**.
3. Choose **Add custom connector**, name it Demand Now and paste the URL.
4. Click **Connect**, log in to Demand Now and choose **Allow**.

# Connect ChatGPT

1. Copy the **Connector URL** from **Settings > AI assistants** in Demand Now.
2. In ChatGPT, open **Settings**, then **Apps & Connectors**. If you don't see **Create**, turn on Developer mode under **Advanced**.
3. Choose **Create**, name it Demand Now, paste the URL and pick **OAuth**.
4. Log in to Demand Now and choose **Allow**.

# What to ask

Try something like: "Acme Ltd owes us $7,800 on invoice 1042, due June 3. Start a demand in Demand Now."

The assistant can:

- check where a debt would go (CRT, Small Claims or a lawyer) and how long is left on the limitation period,
- create a new matter from the details you give it,
- list your matters and their status, and
- show one matter's steps, deadlines and next step.

A matter added by an assistant shows a banner asking you to check the debtor's legal name, address and the amount before you send. If you don't have an active plan, the assistant's matter is saved as a [one-off demand](/help/one-off-demand) instead.

# Disconnect

Connected assistants are listed under **Settings > AI assistants**. Click **Disconnect** to end that assistant's access straight away.
