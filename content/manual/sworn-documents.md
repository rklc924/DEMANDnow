Some court documents only count if you sign them in front of an official. An **affidavit**, such as the affidavit in support of a garnishing order, has to be sworn or affirmed before a **commissioner for taking affidavits**. Some other documents, such as certified copies or papers you'll use outside BC, have to be **notarized** by a notary public. Demand Now gives you two ways to get this done.

> **Important:** Don't sign the document before you're in front of the commissioner or notary. They have to watch you sign it. Bring government photo ID.

# Where you'll see it

- **Under a checklist step.** When a step's document has to be sworn or notarized, the step says **Needs a commissioner** (or **Needs a notary**) and shows the two options below it. In Small Claims, this appears on the **Garnishing order after judgment** step.
- **On every matter and lien page.** The **Need something sworn or notarized?** card near the bottom lets you arrange it for any other document. Type the document's name and choose whether it needs a commissioner or a notary.

# Option 1: Use a local partner

1. Click **Use a local partner**.
2. If partners are listed, you can contact one directly.
3. Otherwise, enter your city or postal code and click **Refer me to a partner near me**. We email you a partner near you, usually within one business day.

Referrals are free from Demand Now. The partner charges their own fee when you visit.

# Option 2: Book a mobile notary or commissioner

A notary or commissioner comes to your office or home.

1. Click **Book a mobile notary or commissioner** (on a checklist step, the button names the one you need).
2. Enter the number of pages to be signed and stamped.
3. Enter the address where they should meet you, who is signing, and the best days and times.
4. Click **Book and charge my card**.

The fee is {{WITNESS_PAGE_FEE}} per page, charged to your card on file when you book, plus applicable tax. **Travel costs may be extra.** If they apply, we tell you the amount and confirm the appointment time with you before the visit, and nothing more is charged until you agree. Your booking shows on the matter, and the charge appears under **Billing > Invoices and receipts**.

> **Tip:** Only Admins, and regular users their Admin has allowed to order paid services, can book a mobile notary or commissioner or ask for a referral.

# What this is and isn't

Demand Now arranges the appointment. The notary or commissioner is independent and only witnesses your signature. They don't check whether what the document says is right, and this isn't legal advice. If you're not sure what to put in an affidavit, use **Request lawyer review** on the matter.
