Every Demand Now login belongs to an **organization**, which is your company. The person who signs up becomes its first **Admin**. Admins can add colleagues and decide what each of them can do.

# Roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Admin | Everything, including **Billing**, the card on file, **Settings** (letterhead and mailbox), add-ons and the **Team** page. Admins can open any matter to change or undo what someone else did |
| Regular user | Day-to-day work on matters: reviewing letters, uploading documents and working through checklists. An admin can switch off some actions for each regular user (see below) |

Only admins see **Team**, **Settings** and **Billing** in the menu.

# Add a user

1. Open **Team** in the menu.
2. Under **Add a user**, enter their name and email and choose a **Role**.
3. Enter a temporary password, or leave it blank and one is generated for you. The password is shown only once, so copy it and give it to the person.
4. Click **Add user**. They sign in with their email and the temporary password.

# Choose what a regular user can do

For each regular user, the Team page has three switches. All are on unless you turn them off:

- **Send demand letters**
- **Order paid services (process servers, lien filings, searches)**
- **Add, delete and mark invoices paid**

Untick the ones you want to switch off and click **Save access**.

# Manage users

- **Set role:** change someone between **Regular user** and **Admin**.
- **Reset password:** enter a new temporary password. This signs the user out everywhere.
- **Block access** stops someone signing in without deleting them. **Restore access** lets them back in.
- **Delete user** removes them from your organization.

Your organization always keeps at least one admin, so the last admin can't be made a regular user, blocked or deleted.

# Organization name

Under **Organization**, change the name and click **Save name**. It's shown on letters and to your team.

# Recent activity

The **Recent activity** log on the Team page lists who did what: letters sent, invoices added or deleted, orders for paid services, and team changes.
