# What's coming

Your company will be able to have more than one person on its Demand Now account:

- **Admins** will manage the company: its letterhead and details, sending rules, billing and the card on file, and who has access.
- **Regular users** will work on matters day to day: reviewing and sending letters, uploading documents and working through checklists.

Until then, each Demand Now account belongs to one login. If several people at your company need access, contact [Support](/support).
