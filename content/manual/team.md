Every Demand Now login belongs to an **organization**, which is your company. The person who signs up becomes its first **Admin**. Admins add colleagues, assign matters to them, decide what regular users can do, and can see what everyone is working on.

# Roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Admin | Everything, including **Billing**, the card on file, **Settings** (letterhead, auto-send and mailbox), turning on add-ons, **Run nightly sync** and the **Team** page. Admins see every matter and can change or undo what someone else did |
| Regular user | Works on the matters assigned to them: reviewing letters, working through the checklist, uploading documents and confirming the debtor's legal name. The admin chooses which other actions regular users can take (see below) |

Only admins see **Team**, **Settings** and **Billing** in the menu.

# Add a user

1. Open **Team** in the menu.
2. Under **Add a user**, enter their name and email and choose a **Role**.
3. Enter a temporary password, or leave it blank and one is generated for you. The password is shown only once, so copy it and give it to the person.
4. Click **Add user**. They sign in with their email and the temporary password.

# Assign matters

Every matter, whether it's an invoice or a builders lien, can be assigned to one person.

- **New matters:** the **Add an invoice** and **Start a lien** forms have an **Assigned to** list. It starts on your own name. A regular user who isn't allowed to assign matters gets each new matter assigned to themselves.
- **Matters from your accounting system** start out unassigned. Admins see them on Receivables, and can pick **Nobody yet** in the **Show matters assigned to** list to find them.
- **Reassign:** open the matter. The **Assigned to** box at the top shows who has it. Pick someone else and click **Assign**, or pick **Nobody** to unassign it.

Whoever is assigned gets an email when the matter is assigned to them, and another whenever someone else moves it forward: a demand letter sent (including auto-sent letters), the invoice marked paid, a checklist step ticked, a document uploaded, a process server booked or reporting service, a lawyer review requested, or a lien filing or title search ordered. They don't get an email for their own actions.

# Who's working on what

The **Who's working on what** section of the Team page lists each person with their open matters, where each one is up to (for example "Letter sent, waiting for payment" or "Next: File the Notice of Claim") and when they were last active. Matters nobody has been assigned are grouped under **Not assigned yet**. Click a matter to open it.

On **Receivables**, the **Assigned to** column shows who has each invoice, and the status column shows the current stage. Use **Show matters assigned to** to see one person's list.

# Choose what regular users can do

The **What regular users can do** table on the Team page sets the rules for every regular user. Tick or untick the boxes in the **Regular user** column and click **Save for all regular users**.

| Action | Starts as |
| --- | --- |
| See every matter, not just the ones assigned to them | Off |
| Add invoices and liens | On |
| Send demand letters | On |
| Mark invoices paid and record funds collected | On |
| Delete invoices | Off |
| Assign and reassign matters | Off |
| Order paid services (process servers, lien filings, searches) | Off |
| Request lawyer review | Off |

Regular users can always open the matters assigned to them, work through the checklist, upload documents and confirm the debtor's legal name. Billing, settings, auto-send, add-ons, **Run nightly sync** and the Team page are for admins only.

When a regular user tries something they're not allowed to do, they see "Your admin hasn't given you access to do that." If they open a matter that isn't assigned to them, they're told to ask you to assign it.

# Set something different for one person

Under **Users**, each regular user has the same list of switches, showing what they can do now. Tick or untick a box and click **Save access** to make an exception for that person only. A **just for them** label marks each setting that differs from the rules for all regular users. Later changes to the table still apply to everything you haven't set differently for them.

# Manage users

- **Set role:** change someone between **Regular user** and **Admin**.
- **Reset password:** enter a new temporary password. This signs the user out everywhere.
- **Block access** stops someone signing in without deleting them. **Restore access** lets them back in. Blocked users can't be assigned matters and don't get update emails, so reassign their matters to someone else.
- **Delete user** removes them from your organization.

Your organization always keeps at least one admin, so the last admin can't be made a regular user, blocked or deleted.

# Organization name

Under **Organization**, change the name and click **Save name**. It's shown on letters and to your team.

# Recent activity

The **Recent activity** log on the Team page lists who did what: letters sent, invoices added or deleted, matters assigned, orders for paid services, and team changes.

# Claude and ChatGPT

If a regular user connects an AI assistant, it can only see the matters they can see, and a matter it creates is assigned to them. If they're not allowed to add matters, the assistant can't add them either.
