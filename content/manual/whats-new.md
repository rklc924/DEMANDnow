New features and changes to Demand Now, newest first.

# November 2026

- **Assign matters and control what regular users can do.** Assign each matter to a colleague, who gets an email when it's assigned and whenever someone else moves it forward. Regular users see only their own matters unless you allow more. Admins get a **Who's working on what** view and one table to set what all regular users can do. See [Your team and user roles](/help/team).
- **The checklist runs the matter.** Ticking a step moves the matter to the next step, removes that step's reminders and sets the next one. A new **Funds collected** box records full or part payments: paid in full closes the matter and stops every further step and reminder. See [If the debt stays unpaid](/help/enforcement).
- **Team accounts.** Add colleagues as Admins or regular users, choose what each regular user can do, and see a log of recent activity. See [Your team and user roles](/help/team).
- **Receipts.** Download a paid receipt for every payment under **Billing > Invoices and receipts**.
- **User manual.** This manual is now built into the app under **Help**, and it's updated with every new feature.
- **Add invoices by hand.** Enter invoices yourself alongside the ones from your accounting system. See [Receivables dashboard](/help/receivables).
- **Claude and ChatGPT.** Connect an AI assistant to start demands from a chat. See [Claude and ChatGPT](/help/ai-assistants).
- **Support, terms and privacy pages.**
- **Builders liens.** Work out your lien deadlines, prepare Form 5, have us file it, and pull a post-registration title search. See [Builders liens](/help/builders-liens).
- **Legal name and address check, and company searches.** Confirm who you're pursuing before you send. See [Demand letters](/help/demand-letters).
- **Certificates of Service** pre-filled for companies and individuals.
- **Process server booking** from the matter page.
- **Email and calendar connection, Calendar page, document checks and tick-box checklists.**
- **Plans, card on file and one-off demands.**
