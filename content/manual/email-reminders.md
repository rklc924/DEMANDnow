Demand Now emails you the steps to take on each matter, and reminds you before each deadline. Open **Reminders** in the menu to choose what you get. Each person on your team has their own settings.

# What you're emailed about

You get at most one email a day, listing everything that needs your attention. Each item says what's due, when, what to do, and has an **Open the matter** link.

- **Deadlines** get a reminder **3 days before** and **1 day before** by default. You can also choose **7 days before** and **On the day**.
- **New steps** are emailed on the day they open. For example, the day after a demand letter's deadline to pay passes, you're told it's time to start your claim.

The software works out the dates from what you've entered, so keep each matter up to date: mark invoices paid, tick checklist steps, and enter the date you served the Notice of Claim. Once a matter is paid or a step is done, its reminders stop.

| Group | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Demand letters | An invoice passing your threshold and ready for a demand letter, and the letter's deadline to pay |
| Starting a claim | The next step once the deadline to pay passes (a CRT or Small Claims claim, or **Request lawyer review** for larger debts), and a check 14 days later that your Notice of Claim is filed and served |
| Service and the Reply | After you enter the date you served the Notice of Claim: when the Reply is due, and when you can apply for a default order |
| Limitation dates | A warning 60 days before the limitation date, and reminders before the date itself, while no claim has been started |
| Builders liens | The last day to file a claim of lien, start an action, respond to a notice to commence an action, and make a trust claim |

> **Important:** reminders are legal information about BC procedure, not legal advice. Always check a date against the court or tribunal's own rules. For advice on a matter, use **Request lawyer review**.

# Choose your reminders

1. Open **Reminders** in the menu (or **Email reminders** on the Calendar or a matter page).
2. Under **Your reminders**, tick **Email me reminders** to turn them on or off.
3. Under **Which matters**, choose **Every matter in the organization** or **Only matters assigned to me**. If your admin decides this for regular users, you'll see a note instead.
4. Under **Remind me before a deadline**, tick when you want reminders.
5. Under **What to remind me about**, untick any group you don't want.
6. Click **Save reminder settings**.

**Send me a test email** sends you an example right away, listing what's coming up for you.

**Coming up in the next 60 days** lists every date that applies to you and the days your reminders will go out.

Reminder emails go only to a confirmed email address, and only while your organization has a plan. See [Plans and billing](/help/billing).

# Team reminder settings (admins)

Admins see **Team reminder settings** on the same page:

- **Send reminder emails to people on this team** turns reminders on or off for everyone.
- **Regular users are reminded about** either **Only the matters assigned to them** or **Every matter**. With **Every matter**, each person can still narrow it to their own.
- Click **Save team settings**.

Each matter is assigned to the first person who works on it, such as by adding the invoice or sending the letter. Admins always get reminders for matters nobody is assigned to, or whose assigned user has been blocked or deleted, so nothing is missed.

**Who gets reminders** shows each person on your team and whether they're getting reminders. See [Your team and user roles](/help/team).

# Stop reminder emails

Every reminder email has two links at the bottom: **Choose which reminders you get** opens this page, and **Stop all reminder emails** turns them off for you after you confirm. Your matters, the Calendar and any reminders in your own calendar app aren't affected.
